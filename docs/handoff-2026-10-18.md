## Why the venue changed

the author to attest that AI generated no substantive content, analysis or data. The manuscript
declared that an AI assistant helped implement and debug the analysis pipeline. Those statements
conflicted.

TeMA publishes **no AI policy** — the official `Format_TeMA_paper_2026.docx` has no AI clause and
`about/editorialPolicies` returns 404. The author therefore **removed the AI declaration** from the
manuscript on 2026-10-18. The text was accurate while it stood and is preserved verbatim in
`docs/submission-trp.md`.
