ABSTRACT (Cambria 10, UPPERCASE, ITALIC)

Context and background

(in Cambria normal 10pt) Author(s) is (are) invited to summarize clearly in this section the content of the manuscript by describing the context and background of the topic, and any hypothesis or assumptions to be verified.

In case a manuscript is submitted in French or Arabic, the Abstract is extended to more than 300 and less than 500 words in order to encompass the content. The Title is provided first in English. The title and subtitle in French or Arabic are provided in a separate page following the first page. The abstract of any submitted manuscript to the AJLP&amp;GS journal shall be provided in MS word format in single spaced in Cambria 10 pt, justified. This section is followed by the below parts.

Goal and Objectives:

Author(s) is (are) invited to present an extended description of the goal and objectives behind the contribution.

Methodology:

An extended overview of the followed methodology shall be presented.

Results:

The overall results shall be presented in order to show the major findings of the manuscript.

The extended abstract should not exceed 500 words and should not include equations or references. If you use abbreviations in the paper, e.g., IS, you are invited to use the full meaning of the abbreviation in the abstract, e.g., Information Systems. The electronic readability of your submitted copy is of critical importance. This author guideline developed for authors is serving as a template format for every submitted paper.

The abstract is made in a separated page.

INTRODUCTION

The paper should be limited to no more than twenty (20) multiple-spaced pages (1.2) including the references, figures, diagrams, etc. The main text shall be written by Cambria 12 pt, justified. Manuscripts exceeding 20 pages may be rejected by the Editor. The margins of the paper are set to: Top (2)/ bottom (1.5)/ Right (1.5)/ Left (1,5).

The final revised paper should be directed to the attention of the Editor in word format into a form of one columns starting at the second page with 1cm of spacing through the web journal platform or via the email : nelga@iav.ac.ma.

Submission process

The process of submitting your paper follows the below steps:

Establish your paper according to the template format of the journal,

Remove the authors’ names and their affiliations from the first page,

The authors’ information are added to the final version of the paper,

Click « new submission »

A new page is shown, select your language,

Add an eventual comment in the reserved area,

Click « save and continue »,

In the next page, click « choose file »,

Select your template file in Word format,

click « upload » and « save and continue »

Follow the upcoming instructions to finalize your submission process.

CONCLUSIONS

Conclude on the results of your study/paper.

ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Author/authors are invited to acknowledge the efforts made by others to facilitate the study or to thank any contributors in the paper.

FUNDING

For submitted papers beyond May 2020, author or authors are invited to add this section to indicate the source of funding the findings of the presented manuscripts. Otherwise, the author or authors shall just indicate – NO FUNDING -.

The funding includes any form of support that could be: budget, providing data, ensuring institutional support, financing internship, providing laboratory equipment for experimentation, … etc.

AUTHOR CONTRIBUTIONS:

Each contributor to the manuscript is invited to present shortly his role (investigator, writer, reviewer, field task, laboratory task, ..etc.) in the paper
