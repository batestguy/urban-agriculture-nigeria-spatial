## What went wrong, in one paragraph

The pre-submission anonymity check only read `word/document.xml`. The body of the review copy was
clean, but Pandoc writes the front-matter author into `docProps/core.xml` (Word's File → Info →
Author) and the affiliation into `docProps/custom.xml`, and neither was scanned. The supplementary
file — also uploaded "for review" — carried the name as a visible byline as well. The journal
caught the first; nobody had caught the second. Full detail: D-17, D-18.

  Conditions — that is a terms acceptance and needs the author's explicit yes.
